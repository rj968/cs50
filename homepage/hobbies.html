What are hobbies?

Hobbies are enjoyable activities people pursue in their free time for relaxation, pleasure, and personal growth. They can be artistic, intellectual, physical, or social, and they often provide benefits such as stress reduction, improved creativity, and enhanced focus. Engaging in hobbies like drawing, reading, or sports can help you de-stress, learn new skills, and even discover new passions.
